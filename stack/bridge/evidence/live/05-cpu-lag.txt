NO GROWING LAG. The gap distribution is tight at both rates and the maximum gap
is tens of milliseconds, not seconds: rosbridge's default queue is not building a
backlog at 20 Hz of a 165-byte message. The 50-70 ms outliers are single missed
browser ticks, not accumulation -- the mean stays at the nominal 50 ms.

THE COST IS ONE CORE, and it is the subscriptions, not the joystick: 20 Hz out is
nothing beside ~50 Hz of /quad_state at 2.6 kB serialised to JSON. 84.3 % and
79.0 % of a single core on an 8-vCPU guest, which matches the 70-90 % measured
for #62 (kennel_console/dashboard.md) with the same eight subscriptions.

WHAT IS NOT ABOUT THE BRIDGE: at rate 1.0 the ROBOT is worse off -- 0.220 m/s
against the same 0.5 m/s command, and a body sagging to 0.221 m where at 0.5 it
holds 0.289 m. That is the simulator failing to keep up with wall time on this
guest, which is why the composed default is 0.75 and why #21 measured at 0.5. The
bridge is slightly CHEAPER at 1.0 because the sim is delivering fewer sim-seconds
of data per wall second, not because anything got better.
